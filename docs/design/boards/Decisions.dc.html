Decisions for the developer

What to know before building: grids, widths, spacing, the Encadré, and the accessibility calls that depart from the admin defaults.

Layout

Container

max-w-7xl (1280) centered, padding 16 / 24 / 32, so content is 328 / 720 / 960 / 1216 wide. Grid: 4 columns under 768 (gap 16), 8 from 768 (gap 24), 12 from 1024 (gap 24).

Home hero

Two equal columns (gap 64) from 1024, stacked below with the text first. The text column is centred vertically against the featured card.

Dernières publications

At 1216: a 596 px large card on the left, a 2 × 2 grid of standard cards on the right (6 + 6 columns). Below 1024 the large card goes full width, followed by four compact items.

Les plus lus

Five columns of numbered items from 1024; a single list below (40 px number, then title and date). Numbers are decorative, the <ol> carries the order.

Category rows

Four standard cards per row from 1024, two at tablet. Under 768 the row scrolls inside itself: cards 248 px wide, scroll-snap, bleeding to the screen edge. The page never scrolls sideways.

Tous les articles

Three columns from 1024, two at tablet, a compact list on mobile. « Voir plus » appends in place.

Section spacing

80 px between sections (56 mobile). Section header: 2 px rule, 12 px, title, then 24 px to the content.

Article page

Widths

Header block 840, prose column 608 (about 68 characters), cover 1120 and full-bleed on mobile. Header is centred from 768 and left-aligned on mobile. No sidebar.

Reading

Newsreader 20 / 1.7 on desktop, 18 / 1.65 on tablet and mobile. Paragraph gap 28 (24 mobile). Links underlined in accent-600.

Tools bar

Audio slot and share buttons sit between two hairlines under the header. On mobile the audio control goes full width and the four share buttons become 44 × 44 icon squares.

Images and video

Figures keep the 608 column with a 13 px caption. Video is a 16:9 wrapper (aspect-ratio) around the iframe, never a fixed height.

Encadré

HTML

Authors only add the wrapper: <div class="callout-box">…</div>. Inside, any paragraphs, one optional h4, and lists. Tinted 50 background, 1 px accent-200 edge, 6 px accent-600 left edge, square corners.

Label

The word « Encadré » comes from ::before, so editors never type it. It breaks 32 px out of the prose column on desktop and runs edge to edge on mobile.

Encadré markup

<div class="callout-box">
  <h4>Trois questions avant de perdre patience</h4>
  <ul>
    <li>Qui est censé décider ?</li>
    <li>Quel délai avait-on promis ?</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Aucune ne remplace un bulletin de vote.</p>
</div>

Cards and images

One ratio

3:2 for every card, the hero card and thumbnails included. 16:9 only for the article cover and video, 1:1 for the avatar. Always object-fit: cover with aspect-ratio set.

Which card

Hero: one per page, home only. Large: lead item of a list. Standard: grids and rows. Compact: dense lists and mobile. Numbered: rankings. Row card: mobile scroll rows.

Category chips

Plain labels in V2, never links. Shown on hero and large cards and on article headers, not on standard cards.

Accessibility

Grey medium

4.1:1 on white, so it is used for icons, borders and disabled states only. Dates, captions and metadata use grey dark (6.0:1).

Alerts

Backgrounds 50, borders 600, text 700 (yellow 800). Green-600 and yellow-600 text fail AA (3.3:1 and 2.9:1), red-600 on red-50 is 4.4:1.

Inputs and focus

Input borders use grey medium (3:1 rule for controls); grey light is decorative. Focus ring is 2 px accent-600 with 2 px offset on every interactive element. Tap targets are at least 44 × 44.

Cookie banner

Accepter and Refuser share one style so refusing is as easy as accepting. Personnaliser is a ghost button.

Implementation

Server rendering

Every layout above is CSS only (grid, media queries, scroll-snap, aspect-ratio). Search highlights are server-rendered <mark>. JS only adds the copied state, the audio player and « Voir plus ».

Fonts

Google Fonts: Inter 400 to 700 and Newsreader 400, 600, italic 400 with opsz. Preload the roman files, font-display: swap, fallbacks Georgia and system-ui.

Mobile navbar

The sticky bar is 56 px: wordmark and « S’abonner ». The contextual link drops to a second row that scrolls away.

Icons

Lucide for UI. Current Lucide releases carry no brand glyphs, so Facebook, X, LinkedIn and YouTube here are stand-ins: swap in Simple Icons or official SVGs at 20 px.

Labels

Copy is exactly as briefed, including « A propos de ma pomme »; correct French would be « À propos ». The email template uses Georgia when Newsreader is not available.
