Randy Donny · Public site · Design system V2

Components: article

Share, audio slot, preview banner, end-of-article call to action, previous and next, the Encadré callout and the toast.

Share buttons

Icon and label on desktop, 44 × 44 icon squares on mobile. « Copier le lien » swaps to a green « Lien copié » state for two seconds and fires the toast; the change is also announced through a live region.

« Écouter l’article » slot

The text-to-speech feature is undecided, so this is a slot with two states: idle (play, label, duration) and a minimal player (pause, progress, elapsed / total). Anything that can render inside a 54 px bar can replace it.

Preview banner

Only shown to the logged-in author on unpublished articles. It sits above the navbar and scrolls away; yellow-50, yellow-600 rule, yellow-800 text.

End-of-article call to action

Small on purpose: one line, one secondary button. It links to the subscription block on the home page rather than repeating the form.

Previous and next

Two cells side by side on desktop, stacked on mobile, each with an overline, the serif title and the date. Wraps the whole cell in one link.
