Randy Donny · Public site · Design system V2

Components: article cards

Four cards that share one visual language: 3:2 image, Inter throughout for title and meta, sized down from V2.1 so real (longer) French titles don’t overwhelm the card. Only their size and the amount of text change.

Large

The lead item of a list — home page and mobile lists. No standalone featured/hero card in V2.1: the home banner carries no article, just the tagline and search.

Standard, compact and row

Standard: grids and rows, 18 px title (20 px in the three-column grid). Compact: dense lists and the whole mobile experience. Row: mobile scroll rows only. No numbered/ranking card: « Les plus lus » was removed from the home page.
