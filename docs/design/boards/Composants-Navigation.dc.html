Randy Donny · Public site · Design system V2

Components: navigation and discovery

Buttons, navbar, footer, section header, search, labels, pagination, empty and loading states. All controls are real buttons, links and inputs with visible focus and 44 px minimum targets.

Buttons

Primary for the single main action of a view (« S’abonner », « Accepter »). Secondary for alternatives (« Voir plus », « Précédent »). Ghost for tertiary text actions. Height 44 (48 or 52 inside forms), radius 4, Inter 600 15. Loading keeps the label and width so nothing jumps.

Navbar

Sticky, 68 px tall, hairline below. The left link is contextual: « A propos de ma pomme » on the home page, « Revenir à la page d’accueil » everywhere else. « S’abonner » scrolls to #abonnement on the home page and links back to it elsewhere. The 2 px shadow appears only once the page has scrolled.

Mobile. The sticky bar stays at 56 px (wordmark, « S’abonner »). The contextual link drops to a second row that scrolls away, so a long label never squeezes the bar.

Footer

Social links, « Politique de confidentialité », copyright and a developer credit line (the name is a placeholder). 2 px primary rule on top, no background fill.

Section header

A 2 px primary rule, then the title in Newsreader and an optional « Voir plus » link in accent. Title 30 px on desktop, 26 px on mobile. Use one per section, never nested.

Search bar

Large (64 px, 56 on mobile) is the call to action of the home hero, with a visible « Rechercher » button on desktop. Compact (48 px) sits above search results. Every input has a real label, hidden visually.

Category chip and label

Non-clickable in V2, so they carry no hover, focus or pointer cursor. Accent-700 on accent-50 gives 7.7:1. Shown on the hero card, the large card, article headers and search results, not on standard cards.

« Voir plus » and pagination

« Voir plus » appends items in place on the home page (aria-live region announces the new count). Pagination is used on search results: « Précédent », « Page 2 sur 7 », « Suivant ». At the ends the unavailable button is disabled, not hidden.

Empty state and skeletons

The empty state says one thing and keeps the search bar above it usable. Skeletons reproduce the exact box of the card (same 3:2 image, same line counts), use grey subtle, and do not animate under reduced motion.
