Components: subscription and consent

The subscription block with all its states, and the cookie banner. Copy is exactly as briefed.

Subscription block (#abonnement)

A full-width band tinted accent-50 with hairline accent-200 rules. Title, one line of description, an email field with a visible label, the primary button and a consent notice that links to the privacy policy. The anchor target is the section itself; the sticky navbar offsets it with scroll-padding.

States

Errors sit under the field with an icon and role="alert"; the input border turns red-600 and doubles to 2 px so colour is not the only cue. Success replaces the form and is announced as a status.

Cookie consent banner

Google Analytics only. « Accepter » and « Refuser » share one style so refusing is as easy as accepting; « Personnaliser » is a ghost button. It sits at the bottom of the viewport and never covers the navbar.
